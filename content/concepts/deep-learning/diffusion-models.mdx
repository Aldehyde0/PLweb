:::field{path="corePrinciple" type="paragraphs"}

```PyTorch
t=torch.randint(0,alpha_bar.numel(),(x0.size(0),),device=x0.device)
noise=torch.randn_like(x0)
a=alpha_bar[t].view(-1,1,1,1)
xt=a.sqrt()*x0+(1-a).sqrt()*noise
loss=F.mse_loss(denoiser(xt,t),noise)
```
